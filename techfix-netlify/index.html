    About TechFix

    Developmental Research — Grade 12 Computer Systems Servicing, Paliparan 3 Senior High School.

    What is TechFix?

    TechFix is a web-based troubleshooting assistant that guides users through common and basic computer problems in hardware, software, networking, and peripherals. It provides possible causes, step-by-step procedures, safety reminders, and printable guides. It is designed to support TVL-ICT/CSS students during hands-on activities and basic servicing.

    Scope & Limitations

    TechFix covers basic troubleshooting only. Board-level repair, power-supply repair, advanced networking (enterprise), and complex OS re-engineering are outside the scope. Users are advised to seek a qualified technician or teacher for advanced cases — a reminder appears on every guide.

    TechFix is an assistant, not a replacement for a qualified technician.

    Using TechFix

    Search for a problem, open the matching guide, review possible causes, and follow the basic steps one at a time. If a guide does not match your issue, try another keyword or use the Chat Assistant.
